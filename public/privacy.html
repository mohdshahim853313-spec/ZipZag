Privacy Policy

ZipZag: Logic Path Puzzle • Last updated: October 2026
✓ 100% Offline • No Personal Data Collected

Summary: ZipZag is designed as an offline, single-player logic puzzle game. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location, or private information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

ZipZag does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). In particular:

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need an email, phone number, password, or username to play.
  • No Location Data: The game does not track your GPS or coarse location.
  • No Device Tracking: We do not track advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA) or device fingerprints.
  • No Financial Info: We do not ask for or store payment cards, banking info, or financial credentials.
  • No Contacts or Media: The game never accesses your photos, files, camera, microphone, or address book.

2. Local Game Progress & Storage

All game data (such as completed puzzle levels, stars earned, best solve times, and daily streaks) is stored locally on your device using standard web/app local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you clear app storage or uninstall the game.

3. Permissions Required

ZipZag requires zero high-risk or sensitive permissions. It only uses standard display and audio features on your device to render the grid board and play tactical audio clicks/chimes.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ZipZag is safe for players of all ages, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

ZipZag does not use third-party behavioral advertising networks or intrusive tracking SDKs. If you play via an external web browser, standard browser privacy guidelines apply.

6. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted across the internet, there is no risk of your personal data being intercepted or compromised through ZipZag servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically if new gameplay features or updates require it. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
