Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I play ZipZag?

Start from dot #1. Drag your finger or mouse to connect the numbered checkpoints strictly in ascending order (1 → 2 → 3...). To complete the level, your continuous line must visit every single cell on the board without crossing over or leaving any blanks.

2. Can I play offline without an internet connection?

Yes! ZipZag is 100% offline. Once installed on your Android device or PC via PWA, you can solve all 500 levels anytime, anywhere without mobile data or Wi-Fi.

3. How does the Hint system work?

If you get stuck, tap the Hint button. It reveals the next logical move or rewinds you back if your path diverged. Higher levels grant more hints (e.g. 2 hints for levels 1-50, up to 5 hints for master levels).

4. Where is my level progress stored?

Your progress, unlocked levels, stars, and high scores are saved locally on your device in secure sandbox storage.
