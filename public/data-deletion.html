How to Delete Your Local Game Data

All saved puzzle progress, high scores, and statistics are stored solely on your device. You have 100% control over this data at all times and can wipe it instantly using either of the following steps:

Option 1: Directly Inside the Game (Settings)

  1. Open the ZipZag app.
  2. Tap the Settings button (Gear icon in top navigation).
  3. Click Reset Game Progress. All local levels, stars, and records will be instantly wiped clean.

Option 2: Android Device App Settings

  1. Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → ZipZag.
  2. Tap Storage & cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.

Option 3: Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally cached data and save files from your device immediately with zero residue.
